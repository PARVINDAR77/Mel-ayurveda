# Dashboard Design Specifications (Five Dedicated Panels)

## 1. Panel 01 – Super Admin Dashboard
- **Top Metric Cards**: Total Leads, This Month, Contacted, Pending, Approved, Converted.
- **Visual Analytics**:
  - Lead Source Distribution (Donut Chart: Facebook, Instagram, Google, Website, WhatsApp, Manual).
  - Monthly Sales Revenue & Lead Conversion Velocity (Bar Chart).
- **Recent System Activities**: Real-time chronological activity feed.

## 2. Panel 02 – Salesperson Dashboard
- **Calling Focus**:
  - My Leads, Called Today, Pending Follow-up, Interested.
- **Calling Progress Bar**: Daily quota tracking.
- **Workspace Table**: Leads assigned to current user, quick "Call" trigger, priority badges. **No source column!**

## 3. Panel 03 – Pending Review Dashboard
- **Queue Overview**: Total Pending, Overdue Reviews (>24h SLA), In Review, Completed Today.
- **Triage Table**: Lead ID, Patient Name, Mobile, Submitting Salesperson, Elapsed Time (`Since`), Priority, "Review" action.

## 4. Panel 04 – Approval Authority Dashboard
- **Verification Summary**: Total Requests, New Requests, Under Review, Approved Today.
- **Approval Cards**: Patient summary, prescribed formulations, commercial terms, Salesperson attribution, Action buttons (Approve [Green], Reject [Red], Send Back [Blue]).

## 5. Panel 05 – Management Dashboard
- **Commercial Operations**: Total Customers, Orders, Total Sales (₹ Lakhs), Delivery Success Rate (%).
- **Revenue Overview**: Monthly revenue trend line chart.
- **Top Products**: Breakdown of top Ayurvedic treatments.
- **Recent Orders Table**: Real-time order fulfillment status.
